One line per straight stretch and one per corner, plus one line spoken at exactly the right moment. The gate splits a long straight in two: the pawn has to stop on the square next to it, say the passphrase from the comment, then carry on through. The real skill here is the planning, not the typing. Breaking a big path into small pieces, and spotting where something has to happen in between, is exactly what programmers do with big problems. Later chapters give you tools (loops and functions) to make those pieces even shorter.
