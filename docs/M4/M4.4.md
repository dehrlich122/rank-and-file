# M4.4 · Free play: spec

The spec for M4.4 (`docs/M4/M4.md` "M4.4 Free play" has the plan and the decisions it builds on). Written during the
autonomous run *(Designer, 2026-10-22: "Finish all of M4 … no further authorizations or testing from designer")*, so
every open question is **decided without the designer** below, as recommended, for the designer to overturn at the PR.

**Branch:** `m4-4-free-play`, from the finished `m4-3-editor-rules`. M4.3's PR #21 was merged on 2026-11-15, so
M4.4's PR is on base `main`.

**Status:** built, automated QA round fixed, `/simplify` applied; the free e2e suite's crash is open (see "Full e2e"
below), so there is no PR yet. "As built",
the hand test and the retest list are at the end.

## What M4.4 is, and isn't

| In M4.4 | Not in M4.4 |
|---|---|
| The start menu's **Free Play** switches on: a hub with Challenges, My levels, Shared with me, Sandbox, Skins | Rewards for clearing a set (skins and trophies wait for a design pass) |
| **Challenge levels:** their own listing (`levels/challenges.yaml`), no lesson, a difficulty, "Draws on" chips | The Pawn tier's challenges themselves (M4.5) |
| **Tags:** tier (worked out by the engine) and difficulty (set by the author), shown in the lists and the Challenge panel; the lists filter and sort by both | Tags on the board (never: red and flashing mean hostile there) |
| **Sandbox:** an open board with every ability the player has met | Randomly generated puzzles (a later idea) |
| **Skins gallery:** the skins promotions unlock, worn from there | New skins |
| **"Save to the repo"** (dev only), moved here from M4.3 | Saving anywhere on the public site |
| Free Play is pointed to from the promotion screen and the end of the Pawn tier in Lessons | The mobile layout |

## Decided without the designer (2026-10-22)

1. **The hub's layout.** `#/free` is a directory like Lessons (M3.7): five rows (Challenges, My levels, Shared with me,
   Sandbox, Skins), each with a line on what it holds and a count. Each opens its own page, `#/free/<section>`. **My
   levels stays at `#/editor`** (the editor's routes are kept); its crumbs now start at Free Play. **Shared with me moves**
   from the My levels page to `#/free/shared`, with "Open a link or code" beside it (My levels keeps a link to it).
2. **Challenge files.** A challenge is a level with `set: <set id>` and `difficulty: 1-5`, and no `chapter` or `lesson`.
   It lives in `levels/<set id>/` (references in `solutions/<set id>/`). `levels/challenges.yaml` lists the sets in
   order: `set`, `title`, `tier`, `summary`. Challenge ids are `<set id>-NN`. Opening one is `#/challenge/<id>`.
3. **"Draws on" chips** name chapters: `draws_on: [4, 5, 6]` in the file; each chip is the chapter's name and links to
   its folder in Lessons (`#/lessons/<n>`). The Codex tab lists everything the tier taught (the Codex counts every
   curriculum chapter up to the tier's last). Chips that named single Codex entries were dropped: which tools a
   challenge needs is part of the puzzle.
4. **The tier** is worked out by the engine (request `levelTags`): the latest curriculum chapter that brings in
   something the level needs: one of its abilities (the first level whose `api` has it), a construct its rules require
   (the first lesson whose snippets use it), or its piece. That chapter's `tier` is the level's tier. The level checker
   asks every challenge's worked-out tier to be its set's tier.
5. **Difficulty tags.** The keyword in the code font plus five pips (filled up to the step), so the tag reads without
   colour. Colours are new `light-dark()` tokens `--diff-1` … `--diff-5` (green, yellow, orange, red, and red for step
   5), checked by `scripts/check-contrast.mjs`. Step 5 pulses once every 2 s (well under three flashes a second); with
   Animations reduced it shows steady diagonal warning stripes instead. Curriculum levels may carry `difficulty` too, but
   don't have to; editor levels get it from a select on the editor's Level tab (none, or one of the five).
6. **Filter and sort.** Each list (Challenges, My levels, Shared with me) has a "Tier" and a "Difficulty" filter (any, or
   one value) and a sort (the list's own order, easiest first, hardest first). They are remembered per list for the
   session only.
7. **The Sandbox** (`#/free/sandbox`) is one big runnable snippet on an open 8×8 board, with every ability of the
   curriculum levels the player has solved (always at least `move`), using the lesson snippets' machinery
   (`loadSandbox`/`runSandbox`). The code is kept in the browser. No goal, no stars.
8. **The Skins gallery** (`#/free/skins`) shows each piece skin large (the pawn, the knight "Charger"), whether it is
   unlocked and by what, and "Wear it", which is Settings → Piece (the same setting).
9. **"Save to the repo"** (dev only: `import.meta.env.DEV`, a Vite plugin that exists only in `vite dev`) writes the
   draft to `drafts/<id>.yaml` at the repo's root, and a kept solution beside it as `drafts/<id>.py`. `drafts/` is not
   checked by `test_levels.py` and not bundled: moving a draft into a set, with its hints and note, is a separate step.
   The plugin refuses anything but `[a-z0-9-]` ids, and never overwrites without `overwrite: true` from a confirm step.
10. **Pointers.** The promotion screen gets "Free Play: the Pawn tier's challenges →" under its buttons; Lessons gets a
    row after the last Pawn chapter, "Next: the Pawn tier's challenges, in Free Play". Both go to `#/free/challenges`.
11. **A challenge's screen** is the level screen with no Learn tab: the Challenge tab shows the brief, the tags, the
    "Draws on" chips, goals, rules, stars and hints; progress is the curriculum's store (ids can't clash); a solve
    offers the next challenge in the set, or "Back to Free Play". No crown, no promotion.

## Step 1 as built: the design sheet *(2026-10-22)*

`design/free-play.html` (shots in `docs/M4/M4.4/shots/`, `node design/shoot-free-play.mjs`) draws the hub, the
Challenges page with its filter and sort row, a challenge's Challenge tab with tags and "Draws on" chips, the Sandbox and
the Skins gallery, in both themes, on the real classes in **`src/free.css`** (new, imported by `main.ts`).
- **Tags: take A picked** (keyword in the code font plus five square pips, a 1px frame in the step's colour). Take B, a
  rising meter like signal bars, was drawn and dropped: it reads as volume or signal and is small.
- **Tokens** `--diff-1` … `--diff-5` in `styles.css`. In the light theme "yellow" has to be a dark olive (`#6c5d00`) to
  reach 4.5:1 as text; steps 4 and 5 share a red family and are told apart by pips and the pulse (stripes when reduced).
  `scripts/check-contrast.mjs` checks each step's text on a panel and on the page (134 checks now, all pass).
- **Hub:** a HUD-framed directory of five rows (glyph, name, one line, a count), like Lessons.
- **Skins:** each card shows the hero sprite large (as on the start menu); a locked one is greyed, with what unlocks it.

## The model

- Engine: `levels.py` takes `set`, `difficulty` (1-5) and `draws_on` (chapter numbers); a level with `set` must have
  `difficulty` and no `chapter`/`lesson`. `describe()` gains `difficulty` and `draws_on`. `bridge.level_tags(level,
  chapters)` → `{"tier": str | null, "chapter": int | null}` (CodexChapter gains `chapter` and `tier`).
- `src/content.ts`: `challengeSets` (from `levels/challenges.yaml`, each with its levels in id order); `LevelSource`
  gains `set` and `difficulty`; `findLevel` finds challenges too; `nextChallenge(id)`; `codexChapters()` passes
  `chapter` and `tier`.
- `src/ui/tags.ts`: `difficultyTag(step)`, `tierTag(tier)`; `src/ui/listControls.ts`: the filter and sort row.
- Screens: `ui/freePlay.ts` (the hub and the Challenges and Shared pages), `ui/sandboxScreen.ts`, `ui/skinsScreen.ts`;
  `levelView.ts` gets a `challenge` option.

### The /simplify pass *(QA2 stand-in, 2026-11-15)*

Four reviewers (reuse, simplification, efficiency, altitude) over the whole milestone diff; they agreed on most points.
- **Applied (`c35f187`):**
  - The engine answers a refused level as data (`levelTags` gains `refused`), so My levels no longer reads a Python
    traceback to decide a row "has problems".
  - `ui/tierTags.ts`: the tier is asked from the engine in one place, for the lists and the Challenge panel, and
    remembered by level content for the visit.
  - `listControls` builds its row once and keeps its own state (it was rebuilt on every change, with the focus put
    back by hand). The pieces the lists share (`noMatchRow`, `starsText`, `boardsText`) live with it, and
    `editorList.ts` is the My levels screen only.
  - `content.ts` gets `curriculumTiers`, `challengeSetOf`, `tierHasChallenges` and `drawnChapters` (one rule for
    "Draws on", where a list row and the Challenge panel each had their own); `storage.asDifficulty` replaces three
    spellings of "a whole number from 1 to 5".
  - Free Play's sections are named in one table in `freePlay.ts` (the route, the crumbs and the titles each had a
    copy); the challenge and section routes use `freeCrumbs`.
  - `resetSandbox()` and `SAVE_PATH` replace strings copied between files; `tierLabel` is the one "pawn becomes Pawn".
  - The Sandbox asks for its board once instead of before every Run (lessons already did), and a failed try is
    forgotten so the next Run asks again; the engine parses each lesson's snippets once for the tier.
  - Tests share `ui/dom.fake.ts` instead of five hand-made documents.
- **Left for QA-061** (the Sandbox becomes the editor), since that deletes the code in question: the Sandbox reaching
  into the snippet player for its loading note; `renderSnippet` and the `.sandbox` layout rules; the `sandbox` flag
  that switches "Here it knows" and "In this level it knows" (the scratch REPL's stand-in piece still says "In this
  level"; one wording for both would be simpler, and changes text on every level, so it is the designer's call).
- **For the designer, not changed:** Esc as "no" on every in-page confirm step (today only Save to the repo's; on the
  others Esc opens Settings); unknown addresses outside Free Play (`#/level/<unknown>`, `#/lessons/x`) still show the
  start menu under the bad address, as before M4.4.
- **Skipped, not worth the churn now:** one batched `levelTags` request for a whole list (the per-visit memory covers
  the repeat cost); `LevelHost` as behaviours rather than flags (wait for QA-061's fifth kind of level); `codex.history`
  placing a tier's sets itself; summaries kept in `DraftStore`; the difficulty-5 pulse as an opacity animation;
  regrouping `free.css`; shared page helpers across the e2e suites.

### Retest list

Nothing below needs retesting to merge; it is what changed after the checks were written, or what a check can't judge.
1. **The lists after /simplify:** on My levels with two or more levels, change Tier, Difficulty and Sort with the
   keyboard: the focus stays on the select you changed, and "Clear filters" puts both filters back to Any. Leave the
   page and come back: the tier tags are there at once.
2. **A level with problems:** leave a level in a state the editor's Problems list refuses, go
   back to My levels: its row says "has problems".
3. **Looks a check can't judge:** the Sandbox at your window size (board beside the code, Run under the editor), the
   Skins cards ("Worn" and "Locked" as words), the promotion screen's Free Play link without scrolling.
4. **Reset progress** with the Charger worn and code in the Sandbox: the pawn is back and the Sandbox shows its
   starting code.
