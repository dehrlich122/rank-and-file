# M4.1 · Sound: spec

The spec for M4.1 (`docs/M4/M4.md` has the plan, the licence notes and the
decisions this builds on). It's for the designer to approve before anything is
built. Nothing in it is built yet.

*(Designer, 2026-10-07)*: music and effects come together, on and quiet by
default from the first click or key, with a mute button in the top bar and a
volume for each in Settings. The track is "Elysium" by Karl Casey, credited
"Music by Karl Casey @ White Bat Audio".

**Branch:** `m4-1-sound`, started from `m4-0-plan`, because PR #18 hasn't
merged yet and M4.1 shouldn't lose QA-042. Once #18 merges, the PR retargets
`main` with no change.

## What the player gets

1. **Music.** "Elysium" plays from the first click or key press, quietly, and
   keeps playing across screens without restarting.
2. **Effects.** Short chiptune sounds for what happens on the board, for run
   outcomes and for the menus. None of them is needed to understand anything:
   everything they mark is already on screen.
3. **A mute button** in the top bar, next to Settings. It's a toggle with a
   speaker icon, `aria-pressed`, and it remembers its state.
4. **Settings → Sound,** two choices, **Music** and **Effects**, each Off,
   Quiet, Medium or Loud (Quiet by default), plus "Now playing: Elysium · Music
   by Karl Casey @ White Bat Audio".
5. **The credit** on the start menu (a small footer line), in Settings, in the
   README's License section and in `public/audio/CREDITS.md`.

## The decisions this spec asks for

Answer these on review (each has a recommendation):

1. **The music plan** *(Recommended in M4.md)*: one track everywhere, owned by
   the app shell, built as a playlist so more tracks are one file and a line
   each. **Confirm?**
2. **Volume steps.** *(Recommended)* Off / Quiet / Medium / Loud, with Quiet at
   about 25% of full for music and 35% for effects. The sound sheet (below) lets
   you hear them before they're fixed.
3. **Which events get a sound** (the table below). Anything you'd cut or add?
4. **Mute button placement:** top bar, left of Settings. **OK?**
5. **When the music starts,** before any click? Browsers refuse. *(Recommended)*
   Start on the first click or key press anywhere, and show nothing special.
6. **Music while the tab is hidden:** *(Recommended)* pauses, and fades back in.

## The events and their sounds

Effects are synthesised in the browser (Web Audio): oscillators, noise and
envelopes, so there are no sound files and nothing to license. Each gets two or
three takes on the sound sheet, and you pick. The character is in the second
column; the exact notes come from the sheet.

**On the board** (the event kinds in `src/py/protocol.ts`):

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| `move` | a soft tick, one per square, a touch higher each square in a run |
| `turn` | a short two-note chirp, up for a right turn, down for a left |
| `bump` | a dull thud |
| `gate_open` | a rising arpeggio, bright |
| `guard` (a wrong answer) | a low buzz |
| `pick_up` (gem, plank) | a bright blip |
| `bridge` | a short rising sweep, then a knock |
| `capture` | a crunchy two-part hit |
| `read` (a rune) | a quick scan: a fast rising flutter |
| `lost`, `fall`, `crush` | one falling tone each: a drop, a long fall, a slam |
| a waypoint crossed | a clean ping |

**Outcomes:**

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| Run complete | a short fanfare, three notes |
| each star earned | a rising ping, one per star, as the stars appear |
| Run lost | a low descending phrase |
| an error | a single harsh buzz, once |
| the goal reached | folded into Run complete |

**Chrome:**

| Moment | Sound |
|---|---|
| start menu: move the selection | a tiny tick |
| start menu: choose | a confirm blip |
| a hint opens | a soft chime |
| a chapter's crown | a longer chime |
| the promotion | a big rising phrase; the music dips under it |

## When effects play

- **Only when playback animates an event forward,** the same rule as the motion
  kit: `Board.animate()` is where the events play, and `Player.next()` and
  `play()` are what call it. Scrubbing, stepping back, jumping to the end and
  loading a level are silent.
- **At 2× and 4× speed** moves are thinned (every second square at 2×, only the
  first and last of a run at 4×) so a long walk doesn't turn into a buzz. Other
  events always play.
- **A cap on overlap:** at most four effects at once. The rest are dropped
  rather than queued.
- **Animations set to Reduced** doesn't silence anything: motion and sound are
  separate choices.
- **Nothing flashes for sound:** no visual is tied to a sound.

## How it's built

All in `src/`, in TypeScript, with no new dependency.

- **`src/sound/`** (a folder, since it holds a few parts):
  - `music.ts`: the playlist and an `<audio>` element. It streams, loops with a
    short fade at the loop point, fades in and out, pauses when the tab is
    hidden and ducks for a moment under the big stings. The track list is a
    small array (file, title, credit).
  - `effects.ts`: the synthesiser (Web Audio): one function per sound, built
    from a handful of helpers (tone, sweep, noise burst, envelope).
  - `events.ts`: the table above, as a map from an event kind or a moment to a
    sound name, so it's testable without audio.
  - `index.ts`: the one object the app uses: `sound.play("gate_open")`,
    `sound.music.start()`, volume and mute, and the wait for the first click or
    key press (browsers block sound before one).
- **`src/settings.ts`:** `music` and `effects` (`off | quiet | medium | loud`)
  and `muted` join the settings, sanitised like the others, with Quiet as the
  default for both and `muted: false`.
- **`src/ui/settingsDialog.ts`:** a Sound group: the two choices and the
  "Now playing" line, from the existing `GROUPS`.
- **The mute button:** in the top bar in `src/app.ts`, with a speaker icon in
  `src/ui/icons.ts` (and a muted variant).
- **Hooks:**
  - `src/ui/board.ts`: `apply(event)` plays the event's sound.
  - `src/ui/levelView.ts`: the outcome card and the stars.
  - `src/ui/titleScreen.ts`, `src/ui/help.ts` and the crown and promotion
    code: their moments.
- **The files:** `public/audio/elysium.mp3` (re-encoded to about 128 kbps), and
  `public/audio/CREDITS.md`. They are fetched at runtime through
  `import.meta.env.BASE_URL`, and only once music is on. They aren't in the
  bundle, and the e2e Chrome gets `--mute-audio`.

## The sound sheet (step 0)

Before anything is wired in, a dev page in `design/` (`sound.html`, `sound.ts`,
linked from `design/index.html`) with:
- every effect above as a button, with two or three takes each, labelled;
- the music with Quiet, Medium and Loud, next to an effect, so the balance is
  audible;
- both themes, keyboard operable.

You listen and pick. The takes you choose are what gets built. Like the other
design pages, it is dev-only and not in the production build.

## Tests

- **vitest:**
  - settings: the new fields, their defaults and sanitising;
  - `events.ts`: each event kind and moment maps to a sound, and nothing maps to
    an unknown one;
  - the thinning rule at each speed;
  - the overlap cap.
  The synthesiser and the `<audio>` element sit behind a small interface, so
  these run without audio.
- **e2e** (in the pre-merge pass, which covers M4.0 too):
  - Chrome starts with `--mute-audio` (`scripts/e2e/cdp.mjs`);
  - the mute button toggles and remembers;
  - Settings → Sound changes and remembers each choice;
  - the music file isn't fetched until the first click, and not at all with
    Music off;
  - no console errors with sound on.
- **By hand, by the designer:** how it sounds. That's what the sound sheet and
  the retest list are for.

## Retest list (to be posted with the PR)

1. A fresh load is silent until the first click or key; then the music starts,
   quietly.
2. The mute button silences everything and survives a reload.
3. Settings → Sound: Off, Quiet, Medium and Loud change the music and the
   effects separately, and "Now playing" names the track and its credit.
4. Play a level and listen: moves, turns, a bump, a gate, a pickup, a loss, a
   solve with its stars.
5. Step back and scrub: no sounds.
6. At 4× a long walk is thinned, not a buzz.
7. The credit shows on the start menu and in Settings.
8. Switch tabs and back: the music pauses and returns.

## Not in M4.1

- More tracks (the playlist is ready for them), a title theme, and a rotation.
- Sound for the editor and free play, which don't exist yet; they use the same
  `sound.play()` when they land.
- Spatial or per-square sound, voices, and anything that carries meaning only
  by sound.
