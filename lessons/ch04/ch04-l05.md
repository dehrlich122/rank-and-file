# Under attack

Enemy chess pieces stand still, but the squares they attack are shaded, and stepping onto one loses the run. A **rook** attacks along its rank and file, a **bishop** along its diagonals. A wall or another piece stops a line.

A pawn takes a piece standing diagonally ahead, as in chess. `pawn.look("left")` and `pawn.look("right")` name what's on those two squares:

```python run
print(pawn.look("left"), pawn.look("right"))
if pawn.look("right") == "rook":
    pawn.capture_right()
```

A bishop attacks the very squares a pawn would take it from, so a pawn can't take it. Its diagonal is as deadly as the rook's lines:

```python run lost
pawn.move()
```
